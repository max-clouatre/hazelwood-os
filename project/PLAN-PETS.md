# Plan: Cloheart Pets (a gentle virtual pet from our own pets) — request relayed from Max, 2026-11-17

**Request:** a virtual pet the girls care for, built from photos of the family's real pets (photos coming separately). Inspiration (technique only, nothing copied): a three.js pet with a PS1 look (half-resolution render, vertex snapping, 15-bit colour with dither, tiny nearest-filtered textures); models generated by code; no baked animations (procedural rig); a vision model turns a photo into traits (fur colours, ears, tail, size) and code builds the pet from them.
Wanted: (1) a pet builder from a photo (a parametric dog and cat driven by a traits JSON is fine; hand-filled traits per pet until vision is wired), (2) a care loop that never punishes (feed, play, pet, bath, sleep; happy/hungry/sleepy drifting slowly in real time; no sickness or death), (3) a room or yard the pet wanders, tap to pet, little reactions and sounds, (4) big touch targets on the family tablet, in the app's style and voices.

## What we reuse
The pixel engine (`wilds/pixel/engine.ts`): low-res render target, fixed palette with Bayer dither, lazy-loaded three.js, adaptive quality, fallback when WebGL fails. The PS1 look is the same recipe: render small, snap colours to 15-bit (32 steps per channel) with dither, add vertex snapping, flat-shaded low-poly shapes, no filtering. No outlines (PS1 had none).

## Phase 0 (this build): one pet, one room, feed and pet
- Activity **Cloheart Pets** for Hazel and Violet. A cosy iso-view room (wood floor, two walls, window, rug, bed, bowl, toy ball). The pet wanders, sniffs, naps on the rug; tap it to pet (hearts, wiggle, bark/meow, purr); **Feed** fills the bowl, the pet walks over and eats (crunch sounds).
- **Traits JSON** per pet (`activities/pets/data.ts`): species dog|cat, name, size, three colours, nose/eye colours, ears (pointy|floppy|round), tail (long|curly|fluffy|stub), snout length, stripes/patches, collar. A procedural rig builds the low-poly body from it (`pets/rig.ts`), animated in code: walk cycle, breathing, blinking, tail wag speed from mood, ear flick, eating, happy hop.
- The five real pets (2026-11-17, from Max's photos): Nova (dog), Leeloo, Aslan, Neo and Ember (cats). The placeholders Buddy and Mimi were removed.
- **Care state** in its own key `hwos:pets` (main save untouched): fullness and joy drift down slowly in real time (about 8 and 12 hours to the floor) and **never below a gentle floor** (the pet is "peckish" or "wants a cuddle", never sick, sad-for-good, or gone). Feeding adds fullness; a full pet says "I'm full!" (no penalty). Moods: happy, peckish, cuddly. No timers shown to the kids, no pressure.
- Sound: small synthesized bark, meow, purr, crunch, heart chime (respects the sound switch). Voice greets by name, sparingly.
- Fallback without WebGL: a big friendly 2D pet face with the same buttons and stats.

## Phase 1: more care and the builder
Play (throw the ball, fetch), bath (bubbles), sleep (pet curls up in its bed, lights dim at night hours); a second tap-to-select pet picker; **photo helper**: in the Grown-up area pick a photo, the app samples dominant fur colours offline (no network, photo never stored in the repo) and suggests the colour traits; shape traits chosen from pictures.

## Phase 2: photo to traits with vision
Before an in-app vision model (needs network and a key, against the offline rule), the pragmatic path: Max sends the photos in chat, Claude reads them and writes the traits entry; photos are **not** committed to the public repo (only the traits). Later optional in-app vision behind a Grown-up switch.

## Phase 3: more
Pets roam on the pixel landing meadow, accessories and room decorating (stars/berries as the currency), more body types (rabbit, hamster), pet tricks, mini-games (fetch, find the treat), pet shows up in Cuddle Run as a helper.

## Rules
Gentle always: progress only goes up, nothing is lost, no dying/sickness/guilt, no purchases, no network. Saves: own key with defaults and try/catch. Old Chrome: es2019, lazy 3D chunk, error boundary. Photos stay out of the public repo.
